App name:
HAPPY LA LA LAND

Short description (max 80):
Book playtime, keep your QR card, and collect free-visit stamps.

Full description:
HAPPY LA LA LAND is the parent app for our Mawlamyine indoor playground.

• Sign in with Gmail
• Book a play slot
• Show your membership QR at the shop
• Collect stamps — 4 paid visits unlock 1 free child visit
• See rewards and news from the shop

Prices are paid at the playground. This app does not collect children’s names, photos, birthdays, or school details.
