Google Play Data safety form — suggested answers

Account creation: Yes (Gmail)

App collects:
- Email address (Gmail) — Account management, App functionality
- Name (parent) — Account management
- App activity / bookings — App functionality
- Device or other IDs (auth session on device) — App functionality

Not collected:
- Precise location
- Photos/videos
- Child personal information (names, birthdays, school, photos)
- Financial / payment info (no in-app payments; pay at playground)

Data sharing: Data is not sold. Processed by our backend (Supabase/Railway) to run the service.

Security: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Deletion:
- In-app: Account → Delete account
- Web URL: host store/legal/account-deletion.html (Google Sites OK)

Notifications: Optional POST_NOTIFICATIONS for rewards/news alerts.

Target audience: Parents/guardians 18+ (not a child-directed app).
